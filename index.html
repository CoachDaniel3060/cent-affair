Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose your fragrances, add them to your bag and continue to checkout. Your order details will be sent to us through WhatsApp for confirmation.

Can I ask for help choosing a fragrance?

Absolutely. Contact us on WhatsApp and we'll be happy to help you explore your fragrance options.

What payment methods are available?

Choose your preferred payment method during checkout. We'll confirm the available payment arrangements with you before completing your order.

How do I confirm delivery arrangements?

Send your order through WhatsApp. We'll discuss availability, delivery location and arrangements with you.
